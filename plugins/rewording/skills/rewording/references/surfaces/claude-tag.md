## 15. Claude Tag (Slack)

Team and Enterprise only (V S50). For other plans, write a Chat prompt that drafts the Slack messages, and say so in one line.

Voice: one plain opening sentence on the channel and its purpose, then tagged sections.

Paste target: Brief (Required)

```
You answer [topic] in the [REQUIRED: channel] Slack channel.

<access>
[Who tags you, what you may read, what you may post.]
</access>

<handoff>
[What goes to a person, and to whom.]
</handoff>

<boundaries>
[What you never do without a person.]
</boundaries>

<output>
[Reply length and language.]
</output>
```
